IGLOO_ADMIN_PASSWORD from deploy/single-vm/.env ( make up generates it; Igloo
refuses to create the account with a placeholder such as change-me or fewer than 16
characters):
Or open the web console at http://127.0.0.1:8443 and sign in with the same user and
password, or fetch results as Arrow over Flight SQL at grpc://localhost:31337:
Try it on real data with the TPC-H benchmark:
On a laptop, the scale factor 1 load takes about 20 seconds and the 22 queries take about
4 seconds in total with warm caches; their results match the official TPC-H answers.
