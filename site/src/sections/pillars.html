Why Igloo

Your tools work unchanged

dbt, Tableau, Looker, Metabase, notebooks and any PostgreSQL driver connect as they would to Postgres, and SQL written for Snowflake runs without being rewritten.

Your data stays yours

Tables are Apache Iceberg in your own bucket, and Spark, Trino or any other Iceberg engine can read them too. Stop using Igloo and they stay where they are: no export, no lock-in.

Efficient by design

Queries run on an embedded DuckDB, on machines you pay for by the hour, which stop when nobody is querying. There are no credits to buy.

Open source

Apache 2.0. Run it wherever you like, read every line, and change what you need.
