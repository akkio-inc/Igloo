Features

The features your data team relies on

All of them managed in SQL.

  • Zero-copy clones

    A full copy of production for development and testing, made in seconds without copying data.

  • Time travel

    Query a table as it was at an earlier time or snapshot.

  • Masking and row policies

    Mask identifiers, and show each team only the rows it may see, by role.

  • Roles, grants and SSO

    Roles and grants in SQL, with sign-in through Okta, Entra ID, Google Workspace or any OIDC provider.

  • Loading

    COPY INTO, and pipes that load new files as they land in your bucket.

  • Tasks and materialized views

    SQL on a cron or interval schedule, and materialized views and dynamic tables that refresh from what changed, when the query allows.

  • Data sharing

    Share live tables with clients and partners over the open Delta Sharing protocol, and read the shares partners send you.

  • Clean rooms

    Measure overlap and results with partners, on data neither side sees.

  • Catalog and lineage

    Search, owners, descriptions, freshness, usage, and lineage from the query history.

  • Workload management

    Query tags, usage and cost per team or client, limits per role, and separate compute pools.

  • Heavy reports in under 10 minutes

    Over as many as 19 billion rows, for about $1.25 or less, on 16 workers. Lose one mid-report and it finishes about a third later, with the same rows.

  • Distinct counts and geo

    Mergeable HLL and theta sketches for distinct counts across days and segments, and H3 for geographic reporting.

  • Result cache

    A dashboard's repeated queries answer from memory until a table they read changes.

  • Clustering

    Sort a table by what reports filter on, and a query reads a few files instead of all of them.

  • Semi-structured data

    JSON in VARIANT, OBJECT and ARRAY columns, read with paths and FLATTEN.

  • Erasure requests

    Erase a person's data from every table, snapshot, copy and cache, with a record that it was done.
