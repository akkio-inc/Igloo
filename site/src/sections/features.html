Features

Everything you use in Snowflake and Databricks

The features data teams rely on every day, managed in SQL.

  • Zero-copy clones

    A full copy of production for development and testing, made in seconds without copying data.

    Snowflake CLONE · Databricks SHALLOW CLONE

  • Time travel

    Query a table as it was at an earlier time or snapshot.

    Snowflake AT · Databricks VERSION AS OF

  • Masking and row policies

    Mask identifiers, and show each team only its clients' rows, by role.

    Snowflake policies · Unity Catalog row filters and masks

  • Roles, grants and SSO

    Roles and grants in SQL, with sign-in through Okta, Entra ID, Google Workspace or any OIDC provider.

  • Loading

    COPY INTO, and pipes that load new files as they land in your bucket.

    Snowpipe · Auto Loader

  • Tasks and materialized views

    SQL on a cron or interval schedule, and materialized views kept up to date.

    Snowflake tasks · Databricks jobs

  • Data sharing

    Share live tables with clients and partners over the open Delta Sharing protocol, and read the shares Databricks sends you.

    Snowflake data sharing · Delta Sharing

  • Clean rooms

    Measure audience overlap, reach and lift with partners, on data neither side sees.

    Snowflake Data Clean Rooms · Databricks Clean Rooms

  • Catalog and lineage

    Search, owners, descriptions, freshness, usage, and lineage from the query history.

    Snowflake Horizon · Unity Catalog

  • Workload management

    Query tags, usage and cost per client, limits per role, and separate compute pools.

    Snowflake resource monitors and warehouses

  • Large queries

    A query over a very large table runs in parts, on worker machines started for it.

  • Reach and frequency

    Mergeable HLL and theta sketches for deduplicated reach, and H3 for geographic reporting.
