Every analyst can forecast and use AI on your data

In plain SQL, with no model to train. Forecasts never leave Igloo, and AI calls go to the provider you choose, priced and capped before they run.

Forecast every metric

CALL revenue!FORECAST(FORECASTING_PERIODS => 30);

Amazon's Chronos and Google's TimesFM forecast inside Igloo: nothing to train, no GPU to rent, and the data never leaves.

Decide on every row

WHERE AI_FILTER(PROMPT('Does this ask for a refund? {0}', body))

A decision model answers with probabilities in one pass, for far less per row than generating text. Rows under the threshold drop.

Your models, your keys

CREATE AI INTEGRATION models PROVIDER = anthropic API_KEY = '…';

Igloo never resells inference: your provider bills you at its own prices. Masking and row policies apply first, so a masked email reaches the model masked.

See the bill before it runs

EXPLAIN SELECT AI_SUMMARIZE(body) FROM tickets;

Answers are cached for 30 days, so a rerun is free and the same. Spend is recorded by user, role and query tag, for chargeback to teams and clients.
