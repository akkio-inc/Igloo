3× faster and 25× lower cost

On TPC-H at 100 GB, Igloo on one machine runs the same queries faster and for less than Snowflake or Databricks at every size from X-Small to X-Large, once warm. Started from a stop, its first run is faster up to Medium and within 15% from Large up.
